# DREVALLON brand specification

## Design read

DREVALLON is presented as a composed, editorial menswear house in review mode. The experience uses high-contrast serif headlines, restrained sans-serif controls, generous ivory space, and midnight navy for the Design Studio and closing surfaces. Motion is quiet and interruptible; no interaction depends on motion.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight navy | `#111B2B` | Header, Studio, primary actions |
| Near black | `#101112` | Review notice, deep footer |
| Warm ivory | `#F5F1E8` | Reading surfaces, light controls |
| Soft stone | `#D8D2C7` | Supporting neutral |
| Muted brass | `#A48A60` | Eyebrows, section numbers, restrained accents |
| Highlight gold | `#D7B77B` | High-contrast hero call-to-action; navy text reaches 9.01:1 contrast |

## Typography

Headings use self-hosted OFL-licensed Cormorant Garamond; body copy and controls use self-hosted DM Sans. Body text is generally 14–16px with a readable line height. Uppercase is reserved for short labels and utility controls.

## Logo direction

The supplied high-resolution crowned knight PNG is the **authoritative homepage artwork**. Its ivory field, crown, knight and navy/gold lettering remain unchanged. The compact logo returned to the homepage header; all headers and the mobile menu use the existing light/dark SVG path traces of the earlier upload, not newly altered artwork. Those traces are not native print-ready masters. The footer's older logo was already removed; signoff and navigation remain, while unverified social-platform links have been withheld. Sticky-header marks fade in, subtly shrink on scroll and respect reduced-motion preferences.

The homepage opens with a full-viewport **charcoal-to-midnight gradient** and the supplied centered ivory-background PNG (responsive lossless WebP where supported). On load, the artwork scales from **90% to 100% over 520ms** with a gentle ease-out and soft opacity reveal. A small animated grain tile sits behind it, and the mark lifts/fades subtly toward the collections. Reduced-motion visitors receive static artwork. The gold collection CTA and secondary Design Studio CTA use refined hover and focus-visible treatments. Garment-specific generated concept photography remains below and requires approval before launch. The supplied image visibly spells **DERVALLON**, while site copy spells **DREVALLON**. Following the owner's latest instruction, do not alter the artwork to reconcile this difference.

## Content principles

The site intentionally avoids invented history, suppliers, locations, pricing, fabric claims, production claims, customer reviews, availability, appointments, or delivery promises. Concept catalogue entries and imagery are labelled throughout.
