# External source notes — DREVALLON redesign

- **Cormorant Garamond**: Christian Thalmann / Catharsis Fonts, official source repository [catharsisfonts/cormorant](https://github.com/catharsisfonts/cormorant); static Medium TTF from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CatharsisFonts/Cormorant/master/fonts/ttf/CormorantGaramond-Medium.ttf`. Licensed under SIL OFL 1.1, with license backed up as `OFL-Cormorant.txt`. The site also uses OFL-licensed DM Sans; three Latin WOFF2 subsets are self-hosted and bundled in the portable project.
- **Supplied artwork**: The original 3690×3834 upload `1000007848.png` visibly spells **DERVALLON**. On the owner's most recent instruction, the image and previously approved header display assets remain unchanged; an experimental corrected-vector candidate is **not live or included in the portable identity kit**. Both uploaded PNG sources are retained as exact backup files in the portable `branding/` folder.
- **Rejected/research-only third-party images** (not used in the refreshed site because ownership/reuse rights or suitability were not verified): Suits, [Proper Cloth](https://propercloth.com/tailored-clothing/double-breasted-vbc-navy-fresco-allen-suit-159745.html); Shirts, [Proper Cloth](https://propercloth.com/fabrics/); Ties, [Prime Neckwear](https://primeneckwear.com/products/navy-gold-brown-gold-blue-red-orange-paisley-necktie-collection); Trousers, [Quince](https://www.quince.com/women/100-european-linen-pleated-trouser?color=deep-navy) (women's product, not a suitable menswear source); Blazers, [Vecteezy](https://www.vecteezy.com/photo/71129542-stylish-man-in-tailored-suit-adjusting-jacket-in-modern-office-environment-with-natural-light) (watermarked preview and AI-labelled); Jackets, [A Day's March](https://www.adaysmarch.com/us/boulay-tailored-wool-overshirt-houndstooth). These paths are staged for research comparison only, not for publication.
- **Active proposed category visuals**: Eight original generated concepts (Suits, Shirts, Ties, Trousers, Blazers, Jackets, Polo and workroom detail). These are not photographs of verified DREVALLON merchandise and require owner creative approval before public launch. A no-logo, no-fabricated-inventory prompt was used for each. Source and responsive file mappings are in `client/src/content/campaignMedia.ts` and the portable `media/` directory.
